# Privacy checklist

The public snapshot must contain only synthetic data.

- No `.env`, credentials, tokens, private keys, cookies, database dumps, or backup archives.
- No personal names, direct phone numbers, employee email addresses, organization names, customer names, or internal hostnames.
- No RFC1918/ULA addresses or production URLs.
- No screenshots taken from a production session.
- Optional integrations use reserved domains and `change-me` placeholders.
- Generated artifacts (`dist`, `target`, `node_modules`) remain untracked.
